Privacy Policy — Looping Dots : Color Sort

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Titoro Games ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Looping Dots : Color Sort (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

2. Information collected automatically

The Game uses the following third-party services. They may collect information from your device automatically:

ServicePurposeInformation
Google AdMobShowing ads (banner and rewarded ads), measuring ads, preventing fraudAdvertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions
Google Analytics for FirebaseUnderstanding how the Game is played (for example, which stages are started or cleared) to improve itApp instance ID, advertising ID, device model, OS version, language, country derived from IP, in-game events
Firebase CrashlyticsFinding and fixing crashes and errorsCrash and error reports, device state at the time of the crash, installation ID

These services are operated by Google. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details; we receive only the information needed to confirm and deliver the purchase.

4. Notifications

The Game may show reminders (for example, when daily rewards are ready) using notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

5. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: We ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings.
  • US state privacy rights: Where required, the Game's settings provide privacy options, including opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Deleting data: Uninstalling the Game deletes your local game data. To ask about data held by the services above, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Retention and security

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google according to its retention settings (analytics event data up to 14 months). We use services that protect data in transit with encryption.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact
