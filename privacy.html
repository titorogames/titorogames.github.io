Privacy Policy — Titoro Games

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy applies to all mobile games published by Titoro Games ("we", "us"), including Looping Dots : Color Sort (each a "Game"). It explains what information our Games collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

Our Games do not require you to create an account with us, and we do not run servers that store your personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your device and, in Games that offer it, in the cloud save service of your platform (see section 3).

2. Information collected automatically by third-party services

Each Game uses some of the services below. They may collect information from your device automatically.

3. Cloud save

Some Games can back up your progress to Google Play Games Services (Android) or iCloud (iOS) when you are signed in to those services. The backup is stored in your own platform account; we do not receive your account details.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details; we receive only the information needed to confirm and deliver the purchase.

5. Notifications

Games may show reminders (for example, when daily rewards are ready). You can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

6. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. On iOS, apps ask for permission before tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: We ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings.
  • US state privacy rights: Where required, Games provide privacy options, including opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Deleting data: Uninstalling a Game deletes its local data; cloud save data can be deleted from your platform account. To ask about data held by the services above, contact us.

7. Children

Our Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Retention and security

Information collected by the services above is kept according to each service's retention settings (for example, analytics event data up to 14 months). These services protect data in transit with encryption.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
